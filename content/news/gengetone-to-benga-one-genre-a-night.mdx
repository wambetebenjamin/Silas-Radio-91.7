Monday is benga. Tuesday is afrobeat. Wednesday belongs to gengetone, Thursday to bongo flava. Genre Night was built so that a sound does not have to compete with itself for three hours.

## Why it works

Mixing a single genre lets a presenter go deep: who recorded it, which studio in Nairobi pressed it, which producer is still owed royalties. Listeners get context, not just a playlist.

### The weekly map

| Night | Genre | Signature segment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Benga | *Golden Strings* — 1970s Nairobi guitar lines |
| Tuesday | Afrobeat | *Lagos to Nairobi* — cross-border production |
| Wednesday | Gengetone | *Estate Anthems* — one estate per week |
| Thursday | Bongo flava | *Dar Nights* — Tanzanian chart watch |

## Requests

Every night has a 45-minute request block at 21:00. Send yours with the song title, artist and the dedication you want read out.
