Diaspora Window runs 15:00 to 17:00 EAT. For a listener in London that is early afternoon; in Dubai it is a late afternoon; in Minneapolis it is 7am on a Sunday, and they still call in.

## The listener board

Our presence board counts everyone on the stream in real time. During the show it routinely shows a 60/40 split between Nairobi and the diaspora — which shapes the running order.

## What we cover

- **Remittance and property** — county-level questions answered with local reporters
- **Family and schooling** — parents abroad, children in Nairobi
- **Music** — one diaspora request block every week

Call in during the show or send a WhatsApp voice note any time before Saturday.
