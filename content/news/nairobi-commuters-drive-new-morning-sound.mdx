When we moved the music meeting from a studio desk to the Thika Road corridor, the show changed within a week. Presenters rode the first matatu of the day, headphones on, and wrote down what was actually playing — then played those records back at 06:10.

## What commuters told us

- **Repetition is comfort before 7am.** The same three songs at the same junctions, every day.
- **Sheng hosts win the 6:30 slot.** Requests arrive faster in Sheng than in English or Kiswahili.
- **Gospel and gengetone are not rivals.** Both peak in the same half hour, on different routes.

The result is the Amaka Early Drive playlist you hear now: 42 minutes of music, 18 minutes of talk, and a request line that opens at 05:15 sharp.

> "I hear my own street in the show. That is why I set my alarm to 91.7." — listener, Ruiru

## How to get your song on

Send a song request any time through the form on this site or on WhatsApp. We read the dedication out live between 06:00 and 08:30, Monday to Friday.
